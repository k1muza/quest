# Kingdom Quest — targeted visual corrections

## Scope and branch management
The unified board at `6622636b30f7434392a275ba7bab9bfaf9490086` was a direct descendant of the previous main (`3cda3c43f5a3924a57efb7f470ffcbbdcc56308f`). The original 41-test suite passed on the exact baseline HTML; a clean rebuild matched Git blob `4a7b92d7348998bde23d52d34bbd0ff32236caf3`. Main was fast-forwarded, then `feature/map-visual-corrections` was created from the updated main. The cinematic research branch remains at `f2fb7667e4536564ce7d44df0abf5be1f8318903` and is not part of production. Corrections remain unmerged.

## Changes
* **Rock range:** deterministic irregular Delaunay control lattice creates planar terrain faces and angular relief with a coherent ridge. Rock material is assigned to high-elevation terrain with grey-blue tonal variation per facet. The original road shoulder, river, and coastline are protected by distance masks. Peak decorative rocks were omitted and vegetation restricted at higher elevations; foliage distribution outside the ridge can vary because exclusions influence placement. No new asset dependencies. This is a stylized rocky heightfield, not individually modeled overhangs or geological simulation.
* **Fields:** construct the water polygon by union of actual transformed water triangles. Intersect complete plots with land, subtract a 9.01-world-unit high-resolution river buffer, then clip crop strips to the resulting polygons. Reject severely constrained plots; validate full convex-hull footprints for grain and windmill meshes. The river route and width are unchanged. The 9-unit target is a visual design clearance, not a real-world dimension. Exported polygons and `validate_farm.py` support other future map packages.
* **Team indicators:** reusable `floating-markers.js` draws beveled colored medallions, high-contrast symbols, varied idle bob phases, distance-driven hops, brief arrival pulses, active-team outline, and shared stop tethers. Six-way formations use stable display offsets around a single authoritative ground anchor, not six road positions. Ground cues remain depth-tested; badges are deliberately above scenery for readability. Draw tethers before all badges to keep team numbers clear. The scoreboard uses matching medallions; old pawn assets are preserved historically but no longer loaded or bundled. Existing score mapping, state reducer and interruptible animation are retained. Winner spotlight follows the winner's actual position, not automatically the castle.

## Reproduction
From repo root: `pip install -r requirements.txt`; `(cd environment && python render_world.py --one)`; `python unified/build.py --bake-depth`; `python environment/validate_farm.py`; `(cd environment && python validate_surfaces.py)`; `python unified/qa.py`; `python unified/record_demo.py` (ffmpeg required). Existing historical environment HTML is retained; the canonical build is `unified/kingdom-quest.html`. ## Known limitations
Depth-aware shadows can be hidden by foreground geometry; the team identity stays visible as a spectator overlay. Closely spaced different stops or moving badges may overlap momentarily, especially at 60 stops; stable six-team ties are independently tested at six regions but not every possible simultaneous path combination. The existing static background is not freely rotatable; close-up images remain limited to its native resolution. Legacy alternative camera artwork remains historical. Physical Smart TV testing is outstanding. No additional maps, networking, Flutter, Game Night changes or cinematic research were introduced.
